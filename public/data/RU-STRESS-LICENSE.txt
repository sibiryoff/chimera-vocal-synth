ru-stress.txt.gz: Russian word stress, built by tools/build-ru-stress.py from two sources.

1. OpenRussian dictionary data, https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary
   (the data of https://en.openrussian.org; nouns.csv, verbs.csv, adjectives.csv, others.csv,
   commit 50e210c of 2021-08-09). Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
   Used for the stress of every form it lists (about half of the file's forms, and nearly all of
   the frequent ones).

2. Koziev/NLP_Datasets, https://github.com/Koziev/NLP_Datasets
     Stress/all_accents.zip (word forms with stress, from Wikipedia and Wiktionary)
     WordformFrequencies/Data/term2freq.7z (word-form frequencies)
   CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Used for the forms OpenRussian does not list, and to rank forms by frequency.

Changes made: the two lists are merged into one "spelling<TAB>stressed vowel" table, a form with
several stresses keeps one, ё is restored where the corpus writes it, the 400,000 most frequent
forms of two or more syllables are kept, and a short list of frequent forms is corrected by hand
(see the script).

Because it adapts OpenRussian data, ru-stress.txt.gz as a whole is distributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to this data file only, not to the program that reads it.
